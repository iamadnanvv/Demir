Who We Are

Spaces. Brands. Experiences.

DEMIR.CO is a commercial interior design and execution studio creating thoughtful, premium spaces that balance aesthetics, functionality and brand experience.

Our Vision

Creating spaces that leave a lasting impression.

We bring design thinking, technical planning, material expertise and execution discipline together to transform commercial environments into memorable brand destinations.

From initial concept and space planning to detailed drawings, fabrication, site coordination and final handover, our approach is built around clarity and quality.
